About El Gallo de Oro

In 1963, my grandparents Lily and Sam established El Gallo de Oro on Calle Fortaleza in San Juan, Puerto Rico. For more than fifty years their jewelry store was a staple of the old city, a magical place where you would always find warm conversation and precious, beautiful things from all over the world. In 2010 they closed shop, and in 2025 I re-opened El Gallo de Oro as a publisher of limited records, CDs, and print editions.

-Eli Neuman-Hammond
